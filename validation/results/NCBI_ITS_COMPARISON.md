# Comparison with fixed NCBI ITS databases

The corrected marker-trimmed fungal and oomycete queries were compared with
fixed NCBI ITS_eukaryote and ITS_RefSeq_Fungi BLAST databases under the same
species and genus thresholds used for MPDB.

| Database | Level | Sensitivity | Specificity | F1 | Balanced accuracy |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| MPDB | Species | 62.0% | 89.8% | 74.3 | 75.9% |
| MPDB | Genus | 94.0% | 85.0% | 93.4 | 89.5% |
| NCBI ITS_eukaryote | Species | 23.0% | 99.3% | 37.3 | 61.2% |
| NCBI ITS_eukaryote | Genus | 84.5% | 86.1% | 88.4 | 85.3% |
| ITS_RefSeq_Fungi | Species | 14.5% | 99.3% | 25.3 | 56.9% |
| ITS_RefSeq_Fungi | Genus | 67.0% | 83.7% | 76.7 | 75.4% |
